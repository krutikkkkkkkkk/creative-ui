# 09 — Copywriting

The words must match the art: **calm, certain and timeless**. No hype.

## Voice

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| Declarative ("Money moves.") | Exclamatory ("Supercharge your money!") |
| Short, two-beat | Long and clause-heavy |
| Concrete outcomes | Buzzwords (revolutionary, seamless × 5, cutting-edge) |
| Confident and understated | Salesy, emoji, ALL CAPS shouting |
| Plain verbs | Jargon stacks |

## Headline formulas

1. **Two-beat statement.** `[Short truth]. [Bigger promise].` → "Money moves. The future begins here." / "Execution is free. Now, judgment is everything."
2. **Category claim.** `[Adjective] [noun].` → "Creative intelligence." / "Quiet infrastructure."
3. **Perspective.** `A new perspective on how [domain] works together`
4. **Transformation.** `We turn [pain] into [outcome]` → "We turn financial complexity into seamless connectivity"
5. **Invitation.** `Let's build a stronger [metaphor] for your [goal]`
6. **Identity.** `The [network/platform/standard] for [audience/skill]` → "The network for creative expertise"
7. **Ecosystem.** `Meet the [Brand] ecosystem`
8. **Proof sequence (scroll story):** `Built on [X]` → `[Brand] taps [specific source]` → `With [qualifier]`

Rules: hero ≤ 8 words. A full stop at the end of short statement headlines; no full stop on longer descriptive headlines. Sentence case always.

## Lead / sub
One sentence, 12–24 words, saying exactly what the product does and for whom.
> "A frontier human data and evaluation lab making AI better for creativity."

## Labels / eyebrows
2–5 words, caps, a category not a slogan: `MODERN FINANCIAL INFRASTRUCTURE`, `COMPARATIVE MODEL EVALS`, `OUR SERVICES`, `GET STARTED`, `MARKET INSIGHTS`.

## Card titles
1–3 words, noun-led, serif: "Global Payments", "Benchmarking", "Creative preference", "Digital Accounts".

## Card captions on paintings
Two short lines: "Multiple currencies. / One seamless experience." "Stay ahead of every / market movement".

## Buttons
2–3 words, verb-first: Get started, Request partnership, Explore research, View the research, Get in touch, Contact us. Never "Click here" or "Submit".

## Stats
Number + noun phrase in caps: `5M+ TRANSACTIONS PROCESSED`, `1.7M+ creative experts`, `$250M+ verified expert earnings`. Use real numbers only and get the client's sign-off.

## Testimonials
Use real quotes only, with the person's permission. Shape: a specific situation, the result, then trust. Attribution: `— Full Name, Role at Company`.

## Microcopy tone
- Empty states: "Nothing here yet. Quiet, like a gallery before opening."
- 404: "This page has been lost to history." + "Return home"
- Form success: "Received. We'll be in touch within a day."
