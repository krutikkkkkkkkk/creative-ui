# 02 — Metaphor Bank

Every section needs one image that **literally acts out its headline**. Find the core idea of the headline (one or two words), then choose or invent the image.

**Formula:** `classical subject` + `the action of the idea` + (optional) `one modern object`.

## Inventing new metaphors

1. Write the headline.
2. Circle the verb or noun that carries the value (e.g. "*unify* your finances").
3. Ask: **"How would a 17th-century painter show this?"** (unify → hands binding a bundle of rods; many streams joining one river).
4. Ask: **"Where does the modern object go?"** (the river flows into a phone screen; a halftone laptop sits on the riverbank).
5. Check it can't be swapped with another section's image.
