# Worked example — Saffron Lane (fictional brand)

**Input:** "Saffron Lane — a D2C spice brand. Single-origin saffron bought directly from farmers in Pampore, Kashmir. For home cooks. Warm, honest, unhurried. Never flashy, never 'luxury bling'."

## Brand truth
> Saffron Lane is the spice brand that buys hand-plucked, single-origin saffron directly from Pampore farmers for home cooks who care where food comes from, feeling warm, honest and unhurried.

## Name analysis
- **Meaning:** saffron (spice, colour, crocus) + lane (path, route from farm to kitchen)
- **Sound:** soft S and L sounds, three syllables, gentle rhythm
- **Letterforms:** S and L initials. The S curve suits a thread; the L is a corner or path.
- **Hidden objects:** thread, crocus, path, lane lines
- **Wordmark viability:** medium. The name is distinctive, but two words is long, so a compact symbol is needed.
- **Risks:** "saffron" connotes luxury and gold clichés, so stay honest and earthy.

## Directions

### 01 — Thread S · Lettermark · ⭐ Recommended
**Concept:** A single monoline thread forms an S. One end tapers like a stigma tip.
**Story:** "Every jar starts with one hand-plucked thread."
**Why it fits:** It makes the provable differentiator (hand-plucked, single-origin) the mark itself.
**Risks:** Monoline S marks are common, so the taper must be distinctive. Needs a heavier small-size cut.

**Concept sheet prompt**
```
A designer's logo exploration sheet: a 3×3 grid of nine different flat black vector logo symbols
on a pure white background, evenly spaced, no labels or text.
All nine explore one idea for "Saffron Lane", a spice brand: the letter S drawn as a single curling
saffron thread whose end tapers like a stigma tip.
Vary the construction across the grid: geometric, monoline, bold silhouette, negative space,
rounded, angular, symmetrical, minimal, modular.
Style: clean professional logo design, solid shapes, crisp edges, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D,
no texture, no mockup. Mood: warm, honest, unhurried.
```

**Single mark prompt**
```
A single flat vector logo symbol for "Saffron Lane", a spice brand.
Concept: one continuous curling line forming the letter S, the lower end tapering to a fine point
like a saffron thread, the upper end slightly rounded.
Construction: monoline with uniform stroke about 9% of the mark width, balanced optical weight,
simple enough to draw from memory, readable at 16 pixels.
Solid black shape on pure white, centred with generous margin, square format.
No text, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no mockup. Mood: warm, honest, unhurried.
```

**Iterations**
```
Keep the S rhythm and the tapered end. Change: make the upper terminal flat-cut, reduce the curl
to two clear bends, enlarge the inner counters for small sizes.
```

### 02 — Lane to Bloom · Pictorial
Two converging path lines meet and open into a single crocus petal (negative space).

### 03 — Three Threads · Abstract
Three short, slightly curved strokes set at a gentle angle, like a pinch of threads. They become a pattern unit for packaging.

### 04 — Field Corner L · Combination
An L forms the corner of a field with three crocus rows, paired with a quiet serif wordmark.

## Avoid
Gold foil gradients, crowns, Taj Mahal-style domes, generic chili or leaf icons, paisley overload, "luxury" scripts.
