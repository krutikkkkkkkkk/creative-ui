# 01 — Brief & Brand Truth

## Question bank
Ask the essentials first, and the rest only if the user has time.

**Essentials**
1. What is the exact brand name (spelling, casing, any tagline)?
2. What do you sell or do, and for whom? (one line)
3. What is true about you that a competitor can't honestly say?
4. Three words for how it should feel. Three words it must never feel like.

**Helpful**
5. Where did the business or name come from? (founder story, place, family, method)
6. Who are your 3–5 closest competitors?
7. Where will the logo live most? (app icon, signage, packaging, uniforms, social, merch, vehicle, embroidery, engraving)
8. Is there existing equity to keep? (colour, symbol, letterform)
9. Any cultural, religious, colour or symbol taboos in your markets?
10. Which markets and languages? (for unwanted meanings)
11. Any logos you love or hate, and why? (for taste only, never to copy)

## Brand truth formula
> **[Name] is the [category] that [true, provable differentiator] for [audience], feeling [adj], [adj] and [adj].**

**Good:** "Saffron Lane is the spice brand that sources single-origin saffron directly from Pampore farms for home cooks who care where food comes from, feeling warm, honest and unhurried."
**Weak:** "Saffron Lane is a premium, innovative spice brand for everyone." (aspirational, not provable)

Check:
- Is the differentiator something they *do*, not something they *hope*?
- Could a competitor say the same sentence? If yes, dig deeper.
- Are the adjectives specific? ("unhurried" beats "premium")

## Name analysis checklist
| Lens | Questions |
|---|---|
| Meaning | Literal meaning, origin, metaphor, translations, slang |
| Sound | Syllables, rhythm, hard (K, T) vs soft (S, L) sounds |
| Letters | Distinctive letters (Q, K, R, G, &), symmetry (A, H, M, O, X), round vs angular, double letters, ascenders and descenders |
| Initial | Can the first letter become a monogram or icon? |
| Hidden objects | Nouns inside the name (Saffron → crocus flower, thread; Lane → path, lines) |
| Length | Short (≤7 letters) and distinctive → wordmark viable; long or generic → symbol or monogram needed |
| Risks | Unwanted words formed by letters or spacing, bad abbreviations |

## Assumptions block
When inferring, list assumptions explicitly:
```
Assumptions (correct me if wrong):
- Audience: …
- Main application: …
- Personality: …
```
