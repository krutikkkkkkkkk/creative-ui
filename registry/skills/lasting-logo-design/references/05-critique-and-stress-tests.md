# 05 — Critique & Stress Tests

## Rubric (score 1–5 each)
| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Meaningful | Does it trace to the brand truth? Can you tell its story in one sentence? |
| Distinctive | Could it be confused with a competitor or a stock icon? |
| Simple | Could someone draw it from memory after one look? |
| Scalable | Is it readable at 16px and on a billboard? |
| One-colour | Does it work in solid black and reversed white? |
| Timeless | Will it look dated in 10 years? Is anything trendy? |
| Balanced | Do optical weight, rhythm and negative space feel intentional? |
| Flexible | Does it work as icon, lockup, pattern and motion? |
| Appropriate | Does it match the audience and the personality adjectives? |

**Keep:** an average of 4 or more, with no score below 3. **Cut** everything else, and say why in one line each.

## Stress tests (run on rough versions, early)
- [ ] **16px / 32px / 1024px:** favicon, tab, app icon
- [ ] **Black, white reversed, brand colour, one wrong colour**
- [ ] **Blur 4–8px / squint:** the silhouette still reads
- [ ] **Busy photo background:** still recognisable
- [ ] **Production:** embroidery (no strokes under ~1.5mm), stamp, engraving, single-colour screen print
- [ ] **Crops:** circle avatar, square app tile
- [ ] **Rotation and mirror:** no accidental shapes or meanings (check key languages and cultures)
- [ ] **Motion:** can it draw, build or reveal in about 1 second?
- [ ] **Look-alike search:** reverse image search plus trademark databases (USPTO, WIPO Global Brand Database, India IP / regional office). This is not legal advice; recommend a trademark attorney before launch.

## Honest feedback language
- "Strong idea, weak execution: the thread reads at 64px but disappears at 16px."
- "Distinctive in silhouette, but the concept doesn't come from the brand truth."
- "Too close to [generic category icon]. Push the fusion further or drop it."
