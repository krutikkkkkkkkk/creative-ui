# 02 — Brand Nouns & Pairings

Brand nouns are the raw material for concepts. Generate many and judge later.

## Categories (aim for 3–6 words each, 30–50 total)
| Category | Prompting question | Example (Saffron Lane, spices) |
|---|---|---|
| Objects | What physical things are involved? | thread, jar, mortar, spoon, flower |
| Materials | What is it made of or with? | saffron stigma, glass, brass, clay |
| Places | Where does it come from or happen? | Pampore fields, lane, market, kitchen |
| Actions / verbs | What do people do? | harvest, pluck, grind, steep, pour |
| Tools | What instruments are used? | sieve, scale, sickle, hand |
| Nature | Plants, animals, weather, landscape? | crocus, sunrise, frost, valley |
| People / roles | Who makes or uses it? | farmer, cook, grandmother, trader |
| Culture | Rituals, traditions, symbols? | festival, wedding, chai, kesar |
| Letters & numbers | Letters in the name, dates, quantities? | S, L, "SL", 3 threads |
| Abstract qualities | Feelings and values? | patience, warmth, honesty, rarity |
| Tensions / opposites | What contrasts define it? | tiny ↔ precious, wild ↔ cultivated |

## Marking
- **★ Ownable:** specific to this brand's truth (Pampore, three threads, hand-plucked)
- **○ Generic / cliché:** used by many in the category (chili, leaf, pot)

Keep ○ words in the list so they're visible, then steer away from them.

## Pairing method
1. Pick one ★ noun.
2. Combine it with a noun from a **different** category.
3. Describe the single form that holds both ideas.

| Pairing | Visual idea |
|---|---|
| S + saffron thread | The S drawn as one curling saffron thread |
| Lane + crocus | A path that narrows into a crocus petal (negative space) |
| Three threads + hand | Three strokes held between two fingers, a monoline gesture |
| Jar + sunrise | A jar lid whose arc reads as a rising sun |

**Quality bar:** a strong pairing is **surprising, simple to draw and true**. Discard pairings that need explanation, have more than two ideas, or rely on detail.

## Fusion techniques (to turn pairings into form)
- **Letter as object:** the initial becomes a tool, plant or animal
- **Negative space:** a second object appears in the gap
- **Continuous line:** one stroke draws both ideas
- **Crop / zoom:** a detail stands for the whole
- **Container:** one shape holds the other (jar holding sun)
- **Pattern unit:** a module that tiles into a brand pattern
- **Symmetry break:** a calm form with one meaningful irregularity
