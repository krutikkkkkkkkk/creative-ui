# 06 — Refine, Present & Build the System

## From AI sketch to real logo
1. **Use the image as a sketch.** Rebuild in vector with pen and boolean tools rather than auto-trace.
2. **Construction:** base the geometry on circles, squares and a consistent angle set. Keep one stroke weight and one corner-radius family.
3. **Optical corrections:**
   - Round and pointed shapes overshoot the baseline and cap height by about 1–3%.
   - Horizontal strokes are slightly thinner than vertical ones to look equal.
   - Balance by visual weight, not by measured area.
   - Open tight counters and gaps for small sizes.
4. **Reduction threshold:** remove details one at a time. Stop when the next removal would lose the idea.
5. **Wordmark:** start from a licensed typeface (check that the licence allows logo use), customise 1–3 letters, kern by eye at large and small sizes.
6. **Small-size cut:** a simplified version with heavier strokes and fewer parts, used below a set size.
7. **Clearspace and minimum size:** define clearspace from a part of the mark (e.g. the cap height) and set a minimum size in px and mm.

## Presenting
- Show **2–3 directions**, recommended one first.
- For each direction:
  - the brand truth line and the one-sentence story
  - the mark large in black
  - the colour version
  - the 16px test
  - 3–4 real-context mockups
- Never show raw AI sheets as options. Show the refined vector only.
- Frame feedback against the brief: "Does it feel warm, honest, unhurried?"

## Brand system
- **Lockups:** horizontal (primary), stacked, icon only, wordmark only
- **Colour:** 1 primary, 1–2 secondary, neutrals, with HEX, RGB, CMYK and Pantone values and contrast checks
- **Type:** a display and a text typeface that echo the mark's construction
- **Extensions:** a pattern from the mark's module, an icon style, a 1-second motion reveal, photo and illustration direction
- **Rules:** clearspace, minimum sizes, and misuse examples (stretch, recolour, effects, rotation, outline)

## Deliverables
- Vector: SVG, PDF/EPS. Raster: PNG at 1×, 2× and 4×
- Favicon (SVG and ICO) and an app icon set
- Black, white and colour versions, in RGB and CMYK
- A one-page usage guide
- `CREDITS.md`: which images started as AI generations (tool and prompt), the fonts and licences used, and who did the vector work

## Ownership note (not legal advice)
In many jurisdictions purely AI-generated images may not be protectable by copyright. Substantial human design work in the final vector strengthens ownership, but recommend a trademark search and an IP attorney before launch.
